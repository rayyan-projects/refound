Everything you import becomes index cards

ReFound cuts your chats and documents into passages of about 500 characters and files them like cards in a drawer. A question pulls the 20 closest. A second model rereads those and keeps the best 5, and the answer is written from them.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Bring what you have

      ChatGPT share links and exports, Claude exports, Markdown, text files, PDFs and images

    2. 2

      Ask the way you'd ask a colleague

      “That bug with the database lock” finds the chat even if you never typed “deadlock”

    3. 3

      Check the source

      Each answer lists the passages it used. Open one to read it in the original chat or file

    Where your data goes

    Your imports, the search index and your question history stay on your computer, in a SQLite file and a FAISS index. To write an answer, ReFound sends your question and the 5 best passages to Google Gemini. Leave the Gemini key empty and you still get the passages, without the written answer.
